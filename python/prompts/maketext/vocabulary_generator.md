```markdown
## 2. AI and Machine Learning Terms {#ai-ml-terms}

### A

**Algorithm** /ˈælɡəˌrɪðəm/
- **Definition**: A step-by-step procedure for solving problems
- **Example**: "The sorting algorithm organizes the data efficiently."
- **Related**: Procedure, method, process

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** /ˌɑːrtɪˈfɪʃəl ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/
- **Definition**: Computer systems designed to mimic human cognitive functions
- **Example**: "AI is transforming how we work and live."
- **Related**: Machine intelligence, computational intelligence

### B

**Bias** /ˈbaɪəs/
- **Definition**: Prejudice in favor of or against something
- **Example**: "The AI system showed gender bias in its responses."
- **Related**: Prejudice, preference, partiality
```

```markdown
### Common Pronunciation Patterns

**AI Terms:**
- **AI** /eɪ aɪ/ - "A-I"
- **API** /eɪ piː aɪ/ - "A-P-I"
- **GPT** /dʒiː piː tiː/ - "G-P-T"
- **LLM** /el el em/ - "L-L-M"

**Technical Terms:**
- **Algorithm** /ˈælɡəˌrɪðəm/ - "AL-guh-rith-um"
- **Neural** /ˈnjʊərəl/ - "NYOOR-uhl"
- **Transformer** /trænsˈfɔːmə/ - "trans-FOR-muh"
- **Embedding** /ɪmˈbedɪŋ/ - "im-BED-ing"

### Tips for Pronunciation
1. **Break down complex words**: Divide them into syllables
2. **Practice with audio**: Listen to native speakers
3. **Use context clues**: Understanding meaning helps with pronunciation
4. **Don't worry about perfection**: Communication is more important than perfect pronunciation
```
